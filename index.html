Signal Drift

A health story rarely stays one story. This is a running record of communication chains — two or more versions of the same underlying fact — and the points where the messaging visibly changed between them: what got simplified or dropped as it moved, and where two versions disagree outright about what's even true.

Every capture carries a record and an annotation, kept apart. How the annotations work, and why each one looks the way it does, is on the Method page.

Revised the story's shape changed — details simplified, dropped, or updated — as it moved Contested two versions disagree about which facts are true in the first place Revised + Contested both are happening in the same chain Isolated logged, but no linked version has turned up yet
