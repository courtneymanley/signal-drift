Signal Drift

A health story rarely stays one story. This is my running record of communication chains: two or more versions of the same underlying fact, or of one illness as its coverage rises and starts showing up locally. I'm tracking where the messaging changed between them, what got simplified or dropped along the way, and where two versions flat out disagree about what's true.

Every capture has a record and an annotation, and I keep them separate. The Method page explains how the annotations work and why they don't all look the same.
